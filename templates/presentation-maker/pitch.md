# Pitch Presentation Template

Title Slide
- Title: Short, clear title
- Subtitle: Optional short subtitle
- Presenter: Your Name • Role • Contact

---

Slide 1 — Problem
- Explain the problem your product/idea addresses.
- Use 1–3 bullets.

---

Slide 2 — Solution
- Describe your solution succinctly.
- Key benefits and differentiators.

---

Slide 3 — Market
- Who is the target market? Size, trends, and opportunity.

---

Slide 4 — Business Model
- How you make money / plan to monetize.

---

Slide 5 — Team
- Short bios for core team members (1–2 lines each).

---

Slide 6 — Call to Action
- What do you want from the audience (investment, partnership, customers)?

---

Notes: Use visuals, keep slides uncluttered, and limit text to key points.
